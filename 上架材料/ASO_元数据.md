## Description（英文）

**Stop drowning in health data. Start understanding what actually matters.**

Your Apple Watch, Oura ring, and wearable devices generate dozens of metrics every day. Most of them are noise. Signalveil is the FIRST app that tells you what to ignore — not just what to track.

**What Signalveil Does:**
- 📊 Translates your wearable data into ONE simple daily brief
- 🔇 Identifies noise: single-day scores, meaningless notifications
- ⚖️ Resolves conflicts: when your device says "recover" but you feel great — trust your body
- 🎯 Shows only the metrics that matter for YOUR goal (running, weight loss, stress management)
- 🔒 Your raw health data NEVER leaves your device

**Why Signalveil is Different:**
Every other health app tries to show you MORE data. More charts, more scores, more notifications. That's exactly what creates health anxiety. Signalveil does the opposite: we curate 5-8 core metrics, analyze ONLY trends (never single-day values), and actively help you mute the noise.

**Premium Features:**
- Daily AI Morning Brief — a natural-language summary of your health trends
- Conversational Q&A — ask "Why is my HRV down?" and get a real answer
- Ignore Lists — mute specific metrics, notifications, or entire anxiety bundles
- Goal Filtering — only see metrics relevant to running, weight loss, or stress management
- Weekly Summary — week-over-week comparisons with one actionable suggestion

**Privacy by Design:**
We read only 5-8 core metrics from Apple Health. Your data stays on your device. We NEVER upload raw health data to any server. AI features use pre-processed trend summaries only.

**Pricing:**
Free tier: Basic trends, 1 weekly brief
Premium: $4.99/month or $39.99/year — unlocks everything

## 审核备注（Review Notes）

```
Review Notes for Apple:

1. HealthKit Usage:
   - This app reads HealthKit data (sleep, heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate, activity, weight, blood oxygen)
   - All data processing happens on-device
   - No health data is ever uploaded to external servers

2. Subscription testing (Sandbox):
   - The app uses RevenueCat for subscription management
   - Premium features: AI daily brief, conversational Q&A, ignore lists, goal filtering
   - Free tier: basic trends, 1 weekly brief
   - To test: use a Sandbox Apple ID, go to Settings > tap to upgrade

3. Core functionality to test:
   - Onboarding: select health goal, connect HealthKit
   - Today tab: view morning brief with health trends
   - Feel Log: daily check-in (3 options: good/okay/not great)
   - Quiet tab: manage ignore list
   - Ask tab: AI chat about health data (premium feature)

4. No login/account creation required.

5. AI-generated content is for informational purposes only. The app does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations.

6. Push notifications: 1 daily morning brief at 8:00 AM (can be disabled in Settings).
```
